    What it can and can't do

    What it resolves

    • HTTP 301 / 302 / 303 / 307 / 308 chains
    • <meta refresh> interstitials
    • Simple location.href / location.replace jumps
    • URLs hidden in query params, including base64
    • Plain-text and JSON link responses

    What it won't do

    • Solve Cloudflare / captcha "verify you are human" gates
    • Run countdown timers or click "Continue" for you
    • Get past logins or paywalls
    • Guess a destination that is only issued after a form post

    When it hits a gate it says so, and shows any destination the page already exposes.

    Runs entirely on your own server. Nothing is stored or logged.
